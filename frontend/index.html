// Overview

What Is IoT StreamGuard?

IoT StreamGuard is a real-time monitoring platform purpose-built for industrial environments. It continuously streams sensor data from your machines — CNC mills, pumps, compressors, and robotic arms — and surfaces anomalies before they cause costly failures.

Powered by AI-driven anomaly detection, StreamGuard integrates directly with your existing IoT infrastructure. Predictive maintenance, energy intelligence, and cybersecurity threat detection — all unified under one intelligent dashboard.

Trusted by industrial operators across manufacturing, energy, and critical infrastructure worldwide.
